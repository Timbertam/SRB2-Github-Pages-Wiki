From SRB2 Wiki

Welcome to the SRB2 Wiki, a community effort to provide helpful information on the game Sonic Robo Blast 2! Here, you can find walkthroughs, trivia, and a plethora of editing information and tutorials! We have information on 1,476 articles as of March 13, 2025.

Remember that YOU, the visitor, can contribute information to our Wiki! Just edit our existing articles if you can add information, or create a new one! It is recommended that you read our rules as well as the Manual of Style, which tells you how to edit articles, before starting out.

Help us be the BEST SRB2 information source, and we hope you enjoy the Wiki!

News

Sunday, April 3, 2022

Hello again everyone. For those who follow these news updates on the SRB2 Wiki, you may remember I mentioned two years ago that I may possibly step down as admin eventually. Well, now is that time. While the Wiki still needs a lot of updating for Version 2.2 even as of writing, I am sure it can survive without me at this point. That, and I need a break after all these years!

Monster Iestyn Talk 20:26, 3 April 2022 (UTC)

Saturday, August 14, 2021

It has been reported a few times lately that both the Modern and CologneBlue skins are broken and showing error messages. So, for now I have disabled both of them, until someone else can fix them so they work again. This should mean anyone who was stuck after switching to the Modern skin should now be back to the Wiki's default skin, Vector, and can continue editing or viewing the Wiki as before. Monster Iestyn Talk 20:35, 14 August 2021 (UTC)

Wednesday, June 9, 2021

A quick heads up since my departure was also announced here: I (MascaraSnake) have returned as Wiki admin. A huge thank you to my fellow admins Cookiefonster, Monster Iestyn and Tatsuru, and of course everybody else who pitched in, for keeping the place in such good shape in my absence! --MascaraSnake (talk) 19:31, 9 June 2021 (UTC)

Saturday, February 20, 2021

Hello everyone,

We still need your help updating this wiki's content to Version 2.2! A lot of progress has been made, but there's still a lot of progress left. Several levels' walkthroughs, like Arid Canyon Zone Act 2 or Haunted Heights Zone, are still yet to be finished, and many linedef types and thing types will need to be updated or added to match 2.2. In addition, we would like to have improved tutorials for how to make slopes in SRB2 levels, including vertex slopes.

For a full list of pages that need to be updated, check out the maintenance category. However, note that there are some pages we need for 2.2 that don't exist yet, like the red links on the list of thing types.

Please feel free to chip in and help update this wiki for the legions of changes 2.2 and its patches brought! Don't worry if you aren't confident in your English skills—we appreciate your help either way and the SRB2 community has plenty of native English speakers willing to fix up your content if they need to. If you have a question or suggestion for how to update the wiki, feel free to ping a mod and ask for the wiki role in the SRB2 Discord Server.

Cookiefonster (talk) 23:24, 20 February 2021 (UTC)

Friday, July 24, 2020

Since there doesn't seem any need for these things anymore (at least for the time being), I have lifted the 24 hour wait for new accounts before they can edit the wiki (it's now 5 minutes instead), and I have replaced the registration questions. This will make it easier for new people to sign up and help out with the wiki. =)

Monster Iestyn Talk 21:07, 24 July 2020 (UTC)

Thursday, June 18, 2020

Just some heads up: Rob and MascaraSnake have left and are no longer "head admins" (not that that role even meant anything anymore, it was a left over from the old admin system pre-2013). Please welcome Tatsuru and Cookiefonster as new wiki admins! I may also step down eventually, but I'll stay on to see how things go for the time being.

Monster Iestyn Talk 21:35, 18 June 2020 (UTC)

Saturday, December 7, 2019

With SRB2 v2.2 finally released, keep in mind that a lot of information on the Wiki will be outdated at first. To get it up to date again, we will need assistance from the community! Please consider creating an account if you don't have one already. If you feel like helping out, the ideal place to go is the #wiki channel on the Discord server. This is where we coordinate the updating effort. Ping a moderator to get access, but bear in mind that spoilers may be talked about freely in there! If you don't want to accidentally have your 2.2 experience spoiled, you may want to wait until you've played through the game before you join. Once you're in the channel, read the pinned messages for some tips on how to get started with Wiki work.

Keep in mind that as form of spam protection, new accounts are barred from editing pages in the first 24 hours. If you want to get to work right away, you can contact Monster Iestyn or myself via the SRB2 Message Board or Discord so we can confirm your account. MascaraSnake (talk) 18:06, 7 December 2019 (UTC)
